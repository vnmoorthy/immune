# Immune — fast-paced cut (≈75 s)

Same eight sections as VOICEOVER.md, half the words, hard cuts. Read quickly and confidently; pause two seconds between sections.

**1 · Hook** (~6 s)
Agents do what their tool output tells them. Immune turns every attack that gets through into an antibody.

**2 · The gate** (~8 s)
Six detectors in front of every tool call. Known attacks: blocked in a tenth of a millisecond. Everyone has this part.

**3 · The breach** (~8 s)
The attacker adapts. Polite wording, an allowlisted host, credentials in four-character groups. The gate allows it. Breach.

**4 · The loop** (~18 s)
Immune captures the trace, drafts an antibody, and proves it by replaying thirty-two thousand traces in ClickHouse. Catch rate seventy-seven to ninety-eight percent, false positives zero. Semgrep rescans the source. Receipt signed. Ruling published. Same attack again: blocked.

**5 · One breach, whole class** (~9 s)
Replay it: it bounces, citing the ruling. Mutate the other families: same antibody, all blocked.

**6 · Proof** (~9 s)
Every antibody carries its proof, hashed and signed. Change one number and the receipt fails. No receipt, no done.

**7 · Stack** (~9 s)
Every decision is a ClickHouse row. A drop-in MCP proxy for Claude Code. Guild runs the remediator. Senso carries the ruling.

**8 · Close** (~6 s)
Detectors are a commodity. The loop is the product. Immune. On GitHub.
